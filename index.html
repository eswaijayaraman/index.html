What is a Factor? 🕵️‍♂️

A factor is a number that divides evenly into another number without leaving any remainder. Think of factors as the building blocks or multiplication partners that make up a number!

💡 Golden Rule:

1 is a factor of every number, and every number is a factor of itself!

Example: Let's find factors of 12! 🍫

How can we arrange 12 chocolate blocks into equal rows?

1 row of 12

1 × 12 = 12

Factors: 1 & 12

2 rows of 6

2 × 6 = 12

Factors: 2 & 6

3 rows of 4

3 × 4 = 12

Factors: 3 & 4

All Factors of 12 (in order):

1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12
